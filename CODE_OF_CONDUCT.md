# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, focus on technical evidence, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publication of personal
information.

## Repository-specific rules

- Do not post credentials, cookies, API keys, or account data.
- Do not request help with unauthorized account access.
- Do not submit confidential exam content or personal course records.
- Do not use issues to coordinate academic dishonesty or platform abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that exposes secrets or personal
data and may restrict participation for repeated violations.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other project communication channels.
